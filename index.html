Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can record payments offline. They are saved on the device and sent automatically when the connection returns.

Is our data private?

Each school's records are separate and only its own staff can open them. Staff have roles, so an account officer cannot change settings or fees.

Can I bring my existing student list?

Yes. Import a CSV or Excel file with student ID, name, class and guardian phone.

How do SMS reminders work?

You choose the class and message. FeePilot queues texts to guardians who still owe, and rechecks each balance just before sending.

Can several staff use it?

Yes. Invite administrators, accountants, account officers, auditors or read-only users, each with the right access.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open it in your browser. If you like, add it to your home screen from the browser menu so it opens like an app.
