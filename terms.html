Using FeePilot

FeePilot is a tool for recording school fees and producing reports. The school is responsible for the accuracy of what it enters and for keeping staff passwords private.

Accounts

The person who creates a school is its proprietor and decides who else may access it.

Trial and plans

New schools start with a free trial. After the trial, continued use needs an active plan. Plans and prices are set out when you subscribe.

Reports

Reports are operational summaries on a cash basis. They are not audited accounts or tax advice.

SMS

Schools must only message guardians who may be contacted and are responsible for message content and costs.

Availability

We work to keep FeePilot available but cannot promise uninterrupted service. The offline mode helps you keep working during outages.

Ending use

A school may stop at any time. We may suspend accounts for misuse or unpaid plans.
