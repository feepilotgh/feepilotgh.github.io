Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This is a plain-language summary; have it reviewed by a lawyer before relying on it.

What we collect

Information a school enters to run its fees office: staff emails, student names, classes, guardian names and phone numbers, fees, payments and expenses.

How it is used

Only to provide FeePilot to that school: recording payments, producing receipts and reports, and sending SMS reminders the school asks for. We do not sell data or show adverts.

Who can see it

Each school's records are separate. Only that school's active staff can open them, according to their role. The platform console shows school names, plans and head-counts, not student or payment records.

Receipt verification

Anyone with a receipt number can confirm the school name, amount and date. No student names are shown.

Storage and security

Data is stored with our hosting provider over encrypted connections. A copy of recent records is kept on the device you use so the app works offline; signing out clears it.

Your choices

Schools can ask us to export or delete their data. Guardians can ask the school to stop SMS reminders.
